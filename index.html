Who we are

Led by grace. Sustained by fire.

Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry is a Bible-believing house committed to deliverance, healing and the raising of a generation that carries God's presence. Under the leadership of Pastor Ifeanyi Ude, we minister the uncompromised word with signs following.

Our doors in Isheri Osun, Lagos are open to everyone seeking freedom, healing and a genuine encounter with God.
