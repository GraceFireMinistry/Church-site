About Us

Grace to walk in. Fire to carry.

Nine years of raising a people who carry God's presence, healing and deliverance to Isheri Osun, Lagos and the nations.

Our story

A house built on Hebrews 12:2

Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry was raised as a refuge for the broken, the sick and the bound — a place where grace meets fire and lives are transformed. For nine years, we have stood on the word of God, ministering deliverance, healing and hope to families across Isheri Osun, Lagos and beyond.

We believe in the finished work of the cross, the power of the Holy Spirit, and the mandate to set captives free. Every service is an invitation to encounter God for yourself.

Our Mission

Deliver, heal, disciple

To set the captives free, minister healing to the sick, and raise disciples who carry God's fire into every sphere of life.

Our Vision

A global healing ministry

To be a global reference point for deliverance and healing, reaching families and nations with the uncompromised gospel.

Our Values

Grace, Fire, Family

We move in the grace of God, burn with the fire of the Holy Spirit, and treat every member as family.

Lead Pastor

Pastor Ifeanyi Ude

Pastor Ifeanyi Ude leads Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry with a passion for deliverance, healing and the raising of a Spirit-filled generation. His ministry is marked by clarity of the word and demonstrations of God's power, drawing families from across Lagos into a place of freedom.

Under his leadership, the ministry has grown into a home for thousands seeking healing, breakthrough and a genuine relationship with God.
